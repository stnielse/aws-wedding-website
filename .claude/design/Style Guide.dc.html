Design artifact 01 · style guide

Louland Falls

Visual system for Kaitlyn & Steven, 23 May 2027. Deep evergreen carried over from the save-the-date, warmed with candlelight gold and cream. Built to be lifted into a Django token file — every value below is a hex plus a custom-property name.

Site structure

Five destinations, one of them the home page. Hybrid model: the home page carries the whole story in a single scroll, and the four things a guest needs to do get real URLs so they can be linked from the invite, a text message, or a QR code.

Navigation rules
  • No hamburger. Five items fit a phone as a wrapping row.
  • Nav is not sticky at the top — it re-appears as a slim sticky bar once the hero scrolls past, with RSVP as the filled button.
  • FAQ and Our Story live on the home page as anchors (#faq, #story), so they're linkable without being pages.
  • Footer repeats every link plus the date — the second-chance nav.
Why not tabs

A wedding site is read once, by people on a phone, looking for one fact. Tabs hide things behind a click and give no sense of how much there is. A scroll with a persistent RSVP button answers "when, where, what do I do" before anyone has to choose.

Imagery

Engagement photos are the whole aesthetic — they run through every section, not just a gallery. Three treatments only, so a page never feels like a collage.

Edge to edge, forest-900 scrim at 35% for text over it.

3:4 · story, single moments

One per section, max. The flourish.

square tile
1:1 · grids, gallery teaser

Used in rows of 2 (phone) / 4 (desktop).

Serve from CloudFront as AVIF with WebP fallback, srcset at 640/1024/1600/2400, loading="lazy" below the fold, explicit width/height to stop layout shift. Every photo needs real alt text — the Photo model has caption but no alt field; see the questions below.

Questions for you — not invented, just flagged

Model gap

The RSVP form needs dietary notes, but RSVP only has meal_choice and a general notes. Is notes doing double duty, or does dietary want its own field?

Meal choices

meal_choice is a free CharField. I've mocked three options — what are the real ones, and are they per-person (plus-one has its own)? The model says yes.

Party vs. guest

Guest is one person + optional plus-one. A family of four means four codes and four visits. Is a household/party grouping worth adding before Phase 2 hardens?

Editing an RSVP

OneToOneField means one submission per guest. What happens when someone re-enters their code — read-only receipt, or editable until a cutoff? I designed the receipt with an "amend" affordance; say the word and I'll drop it.

Alt text

Photo has caption but no alt_text. Captions are decorative and often empty; alt is not. Cheap to add now.

Schedule content

The home page has a "shape of the day" section but no model backs it — no Event model exists. Hardcode in the template, or add one? Mocked as static copy for now.

Notes for the port
  • Responsiveness here is fluid, not breakpoint-based: clamp() type, grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(...)), wrapping flex rows. It survives 320px→2560px with no media queries. Add them only where a layout genuinely needs to reorder.
  • Every value above wants to become a custom property on :root in one token partial; the mocks inline them so you can see the actual hex in place.
  • No JS in any artifact. The sticky nav, lightbox, and countdown are yours.
  • Image placeholders are striped divs with monospace labels — swap for real <img> against the S3/CloudFront URLs.
