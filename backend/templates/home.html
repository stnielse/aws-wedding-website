The shape of the day

Late spring in the canyon

Everything happens at Louland Falls — ceremony under the trees, dinner and dancing in the pavilion twenty steps away. Outdoors throughout. It's roughly 60°F at sundown; bring a layer.

  1. 5:30 Guests arrive Mingle and be happy. Parking is on site, a shuttle will whip you up if needed.
  2. 6:00 Ceremony Under the altar overlooking the canyon. Thirty(ish?) minutes, we (sort of?) promise. Free drinks will follow so just stick with us 😉.
  3. 7:00 Dinner Seated, in the pavilion. You'll pick a main dish when you RSVP.
  4. 8:00 Dancing Until eleven. Cake somewhere in the middle.
